<h2 align="center">The payment operating layer for every interface.</h2>
<p align="center"><strong>One authorized payment. One deterministic state. One independently verifiable final receipt.</strong></p>

Payrail turns fragmented payment channels into one programmable settlement flow. Wallets, merchant checkout, POS, QR, SMS and future applications create the same canonical signed operation and converge on the same final result.

### Why Payrail

- **Omnichannel by architecture** — integrations share one payment primitive instead of separate rails and reconciliation logic.
- **Finality you can prove** — every accepted payment produces a deterministic receipt that customers, merchants and infrastructure can verify independently.
- **Security at the boundary** — client-side signing, encrypted self-custody keys and canonical integer monetary values keep secrets and arithmetic out of presentation layers.
- **Built for integration** — typed SDKs and stable APIs connect consumer wallets, merchant systems, feature phones and future financial products.
- **Operational transparency** — the live explorer exposes blocks, transactions and finality instead of asking partners to trust an opaque processor.

### Product stack

| Surface | What it delivers |
| --- | --- |
| **Network** | Signed payment operations, deterministic ledger execution and durable devnet state |
| **Wallet** | Encrypted self-custody, transfers, payment requests, QR, SMS and hosted checkout |
| **Explorer** | Independent inspection of transactions, blocks and final receipts |
| **Merchant Portal** | Omnichannel payment acceptance and operational visibility |
| **Exchange Node** | Sanitized self-hosted gateway for exchange and infrastructure integrations |
| **SDK** | Typed clients, canonical amount handling and signing primitives |
| **UI Kit** | One production-grade Payrail design system across every product surface |
